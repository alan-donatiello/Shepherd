How it works

From transaction to close, fully automated.

01

Connect

Add a business bank account, a wallet address, or both. Shepherd reads directly from your bank and from the blockchain, with no manual export required.

02

Classify

AI suggests a GL code with a confidence score and its reasoning. Approve once, and every future transaction from that counterparty classifies itself.

03

Reconcile

Financial statements build in real time as you classify. Balances are verified independently against the chain itself and against your ERP.

04

Integrate

Classified transactions push straight into your ERP as journal entries automatically, on your schedule, with no manual export or re-entry required.

Built for Scale

The parts that actually matter.

Reconciliation

Verified against the source

Shepherd checks its own math against a live balance query to the blockchain, and separately against your ERP's own account balances. That means "reconciled" is independently proven, not self-reported.

Fair value

ASC 820 period-end remeasurement

Non-stablecoin holdings get marked to fair value each period with a full disclosure rollforward: opening balance, additions, dispositions, unrealized gain or loss, and closing balance, by asset.

One ledger

One ledger for all your assets

Bank account and wallet activity run on a single classification engine and a unified platform. No more disconnected systems that don't talk to each other.

Audit trail

Every action, timestamped

Classifications, rescans, rule changes, and exports are logged as they happen and exportable on demand, built to hold up when someone asks how a number was produced.
